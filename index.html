The Workshop

The Workshop is an iPhone app for tracking workshop projects, materials and tool inventory, with a shopping list, project measurements, and recurring maintenance reminders.

Contact

For support, questions or bug reports, email software@hewick.ca.

To help track down a problem quickly, please include:

  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • The app version (Settings tab in the app)
  • What you did
  • What you expected to happen

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The Workshop has no account or sign-in.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, it's also mirrored to your own iCloud account.

How do I turn iCloud sync on or off?

In the app, go to Settings tab > iCloud Sync. You can also manage what iCloud stores from iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove its on-device data. To remove any iCloud copy as well, turn off iCloud sync for the app, or delete its data from iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Is there a sample project to try?

Yes. It's offered on the empty Projects list, and it's also available any time under Settings > Sample project. Removing it takes one tap.
