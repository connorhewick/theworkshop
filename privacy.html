Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026

Summary

The Workshop collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, or in your own iCloud account if you turn on sync. There are no third parties: no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is ever sent to the developer.

What we collect

Nothing. The Workshop's App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected," and that's accurate: the app has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party software development kits, and no tracking of any kind. The developer receives no data from the app, from you, or from your device.

Where your data lives

All of your data — projects, materials, tools, shopping lists, measurements and maintenance schedules — is stored on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync in the app's Settings, that data is mirrored to your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. It stays in your iCloud account; the developer has no access to it, and there is no developer server involved.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each of these is optional. The app works without any of them; declining only turns off the feature it supports.

  • Camera — used to take progress photos of your projects, photograph inventory items, and scan a tool's serial number. If you decline, you can still use the app; you just can't add photos or scan a serial number that way.
  • Calendar — write-only access, used only to add a maintenance schedule you create to your calendar. The app never reads your existing calendar entries. If you decline, maintenance schedules stay inside the app and aren't added to your calendar.
  • Notifications — used to alert you when maintenance is due or stock is running low, with a lead time you choose. If you decline, the app works normally; you just won't get these alerts.

Third parties

None. The Workshop has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking. The only network traffic the app generates is to Apple's CloudKit, and only if you turn on iCloud sync.

Children

The Workshop is not directed at children and has no features aimed at them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. If you used iCloud sync, you can remove that copy too: turn off sync for the app, or delete the app's data from iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to software@hewick.ca.
